Our Story

Started in 2020 to fix a simple problem: too many fake appliances.

When Mukesh Verma founded Kadel Trade Link in December 2020, the appliance market in Nepal was full of grey-market electronics with no real warranty and no one to call when something broke. KTL was built to be the opposite of that — a name customers could trust to sell only genuine, 100% authentic products.

Five years on, that same promise has grown into showrooms and regional touchpoints across six cities, nationwide delivery, and a brand lineup of global names like Hisense, Haier, Voltas and IFB — all backed by manufacturer warranties and spare parts support that doesn't disappear after checkout.
